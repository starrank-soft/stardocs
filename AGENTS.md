# StarDocs Contribution Rules

## Change History

- Commit every meaningful content or repository change before handoff.
- Keep one logical change per commit and use Conventional Commits messages.
- Do not rewrite published history on `main`.
- Preserve raw research markers and evidence gaps until the required evidence
  has actually been collected.

## Language

- Chinese paper and article drafts remain in Chinese unless a translation is
  explicitly requested.
- Repository metadata, commit messages, and contribution instructions use
  English.

## Writing

- Keep public articles at the design-principle level. Mention concrete
  implementation types only when they materially support the argument.
- Distinguish state DSLs, edit protocols, runtime implementations, and
  experimental evidence.
- Cite related work accurately without implying that independent neighboring
  work was the source of StarCut's design.
